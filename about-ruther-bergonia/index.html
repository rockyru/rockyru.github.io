How I build

Systems should grow instead of being rebuilt. API-first services, clean separation between platforms, and authentication designed for an ecosystem rather than a single app.

I build continuity in from the start — documentation, version-controlled code, and reproducible infrastructure another engineer could pick up without starting over.

AI features follow the same discipline: the assistant answers from approved sources and hands off to a human when something falls outside its scope, rather than guessing.
