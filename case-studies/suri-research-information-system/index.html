RESEARCH INFORMATION SYSTEM

SURI: one platform
for university research.

SURI (System for Unified Research Information) is an API-first Laravel and Vue.js platform at the University of the Philippines Manila that unifies research registration, document management, reporting, and approval workflows — managing 15,000+ records across 50+ users, and replacing manual spreadsheet compilation with reporting that runs in seconds.

Role Lead Engineer / Systems Architect
Client RGAO · UP Manila
Stack Laravel / Vue.js / MySQL
Status In Production · 2025

The problem

Research administration at a university generates a constant stream of registrations, documents, approvals, and reports. Before SURI, these workflows at the Research Grants Administration Office (RGAO) of the University of the Philippines Manila ran by hand — compiled manually across spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

Manual compilation doesn't just cost time. It fragments the record of the university's research activity, makes reporting a recurring project instead of a query, and leaves approval workflows without a single system of record.

What SURI does

SURI consolidates the research lifecycle into one platform serving 50+ users and 15,000+ research records:

Research registration

Final registration, management, and funding records for university research.

Document management

Research documents handled inside the platform instead of scattered files.

Approval workflows

Workflows that previously ran by hand, tracked and automated end to end.

Reporting

Reports that once required manual spreadsheet compilation now run in seconds.

Architecture & design decisions

SURI is built API-first on Laravel with a Vue.js frontend and a MySQL database. The API-first decision matters because SURI does not live alone: it operates alongside AWTOR, the university's research prerequisite and ethics platform, and is designed to join a broader ecosystem of research systems.

Authentication and role-based access control are treated as architecture, not features. Researchers clear prerequisites in AWTOR and land in SURI already authenticated, through a time-boxed, cryptographically signed URL handshake — no second login, no shared database, and strict decoupling between the two systems.

The next step of that decoupling is RACE, the orchestration layer in development beneath SURI and ARIA, which extracts shared authentication and shared services so future platforms join the ecosystem instead of becoming new silos.

Deployment & operations

Ruther Bergonia leads SURI's architecture and development and owns it through production: deployments, production database architecture, and release management. The platform runs live for the university's research administration — this is operated software, not a prototype.

Outcome

One platform now manages 15,000+ research records for 50+ users, unifying registration, document management, reporting, and approval workflows that previously ran by hand. Reporting that used to mean manual spreadsheet compilation now runs in seconds.
