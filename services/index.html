+06

Questions people actually ask

FAQ

The things every business owner asks before saying yes. More on sprntr.dev.

Is the preview really free? +

Yes. We build a preview website and workspace for your business at no cost. You only pay if you decide to go live.

Is there a monthly fee? +

No. You pay once for your package. Hosting and your domain are included for 2 years, then ₱3,000 a year. Up to 10 content edits — prices, photos, hours — are included too.

Can I keep my own domain? +

Yes. Every package includes a domain for 2 years: we connect one you already own, or register a new one in your business’s name. It’s yours, and we transfer it whenever you ask. You also get a free sprntr.dev address.

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need this? +

Keep it — they do different jobs. Facebook reaches people who already follow you. The website is what people find when they search Google for what you sell, and with Sprntr its inquiries land in an organized pipeline instead of getting buried in chat.

Does it connect to Messenger? +

Yes: connect your Facebook Page and each new chat becomes a lead your team replies to from Sprntr. It’s awaiting Meta’s approval for customer Pages and gets switched on as soon as it’s approved. Text reminders already work, using prepaid SMS credits.

Can you do more than this? +

Yes — my day job is building systems that manage 15,000+ records for a university office. Inventory, multiple branches, payments, reporting: all doable, all scoped and priced separately. See the case studies.
