Questions people actually ask

FAQ

The six things every business owner asks me before saying yes. Straight answers, including the ones that cost me work.

How long does it really take? +

Five to seven days from the moment I have your photos, services, and prices. Most of that week is waiting on content from you — the build itself is three days. If you send everything on day one, it’s faster.

What does the ₱5,000 a month actually cover? +

Hosting, domain renewal, security updates, backups, and content changes — new prices, new services, new photos. You message me and it gets done, usually same day. It’s month to month; stop it whenever.

Do I own the website? +

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and every login is handed to you at launch. If you stop the care plan, the site is still yours — you’d just take over hosting and updates, or hand them to someone else.

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need this? +

Keep it — they do different jobs. Facebook reaches people who already follow you. The website is what people find when they search Google for what you sell, and it’s where bookings land instead of getting buried in Messenger at 11pm.

What if I don’t like it? +

You see it before it goes public, and changes at that stage are included. After launch you have 30 days of fixes. What I won’t do is rebuild it from scratch a third time — that’s a new project, and I’ll say so plainly.

Can you do more than this? +

Yes — my day job is building systems that manage 15,000+ records for a university office. Inventory, staff accounts, multiple branches, payments, reporting: all doable, all scoped and priced separately. See the case studies.
